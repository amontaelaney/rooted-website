Stream 02

Growing & food

Research

A possible school greenhouse pilot

A small greenhouse could offer hands-on learning before a larger campus exists. The school pilot is an exploratory proposal. Cascade School District permission is required before construction or school-based implementation.

Next question / step: Prepare an educational scope and site proposal for district review, including costs, supervision, maintenance and accessibility.

Inspiration

Aquaponics, hydroponics & microgreens

Compare fish-and-plant systems, water-based growing and small microgreen trials. Each could connect biology, measurement and observation with growing food. No system has been selected.

Next question / step: Start with a small learning experiment; compare operating needs, animal care, water quality and learning value.

Inspiration

Vermicomposting & nutrient cycles

A worm bin could connect food scraps, decomposition and soil health. Follow nutrients from the kitchen to the garden and back again.

Next question / step: Design an observation activity and investigate suitable materials, care routines and a responsible adult owner.

Stream 03

Land & infrastructure

Research

Land & property research

Create a property comparison around learning needs: usable space, water, soils, access, existing buildings, utilities, affordability and long-term stewardship. No parcel is presented as secured or verified.

Next question / step: Record the source and date for each candidate; verify allowed uses, water availability and site constraints with relevant professionals and authorities.

Research

Geothermal direct-use opportunities

Explore whether a property’s geothermal hot-water resource could support greenhouse or aquaculture heating. Direct-use heat and ground-source heat pumps are different options; a listing alone does not establish feasibility.

Next question / step: Investigate resource temperature, flow, rights, distance, infrastructure and independent technical and financial feasibility.

04 / Experiences · Draft learning pathway

Eight weeks.
A whole food system.

Planned · draft only

From Seed to Community is a proposed Food Systems Lab. Timing, age adaptations, staffing, resources and approvals remain to be worked out. Learners can follow their questions and change direction.

  1. Week 01DiscoverWhere does our food come from? Map a meal.
  2. Week 02ExploreCompare soil growing and hydroponics.
  3. Week 03InvestigateMeasure water, energy and material inputs.
  4. Week 04ConnectExplore worms, compost and nutrient cycles.
  5. Week 05CalculateEstimate production costs and yields.
  6. Week 06UnderstandTrace food from producer to consumer.
  7. Week 07RedesignChoose one local food-system problem.
  8. Week 08ContributePresent and test a student-led improvement.

Inspired in part by IFAD’s environment, natural resource management and climate e-learning. This sequence is an independent Rooted adaptation, not an IFAD curriculum or endorsement.
